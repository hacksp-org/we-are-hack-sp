# Safeguarding Policy & Youth Protection

> **Our Absolute Priority:** Hack SP is a project made by teenagers, for teenagers. The safety, well-being, and integrity of all participating minors are our primary consideration. 
> 
> We operate with total transparency, zero tolerance for harassment, and an uncompromising commitment to a safe, inclusive, and welcoming environment.

---

## 1. Core Community Principles
Our rules reflect a commitment to mutual respect and support within the community:

* **Treat everyone with respect and kindness:** Be friendly and welcoming. Any form of discrimination based on age, gender, gender identity, ethnicity, race, disability, or language is strictly prohibited.
* **Zero Tolerance for Bullying and Harassment:** Insults, threatening behavior, intimidation, or unwanted romantic/sexual advances are not tolerated. If someone asks you to leave them alone, you must do so immediately.
* **Make conflict productive:** In case of disagreements, assume good faith, try to understand the other side without escalating arguments, and maintain patience.

---

## 2. Physical and In-Person Safety Standards (Events & Hackathons)
To ensure a secure environment at our in-person hackathons:

* **Adequate Supervision:** There is always a safe ratio of organizers responsible for supervision and support relative to the number of attendees.
* **Perimeter and Circulation:** Participants must remain within the event perimeter for its entire duration, unless prior authorization is granted by organizers and parents/guardians are notified.
* **Consent and Parental Terms:** Minors under 18 require explicit parental or guardian consent to participate, along with prior collection of emergency contacts and essential medical/dietary restrictions.
* **Rest Areas:** The event provides suitable rest or work spaces to accommodate participants' needs.

---

## 3. Guidelines for Volunteers, Mentors, and Organizers
To ensure leadership maintains a professional and strictly protective posture:

* **Appropriate Boundaries:** Volunteers and mentors must maintain clear boundaries (physical, digital, and conversational) with minor participants.
* **Prohibition of Inappropriate Content:** Any sexual, violent, or discriminatory material in communications, presentations, or code is strictly prohibited.

---

## 4. How to Report Incidents or Concerns
If you witness or experience any violation of this code, report it immediately. Hack SP guarantees absolute confidentiality and full support to the victim.

* **Confidentiality:** Reports are handled with strict confidentiality. The reporter's identity is never shared without explicit consent.
* **Protection Against Retaliation:** No form of retaliation or discrimination will be tolerated against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.
* **Contact Channels:**
  * **Direct Safety/Conduct Email:** `lucas@hacksp.org`
  * **Discord Community:** Direct moderation channels with local organizers or community reporting tools.

> *Failure to comply with these guidelines will result in warnings, immediate removal from the physical event, or permanent ban from Hack SP's community and online channels.*
